FEATURES

STATISTICS
Trophy room, year in review and play diary: playtime, favourite system and progress at a glance.
RETROACHIEVEMENTS
Entering a system that supports RA, you pick: the standard core, or the RA core with real achievements.
ACHIEVEMENTS
F6 shows a game’s full achievement list – unlocked or still open, right there in the menu.
FAVOURITES
Games you mark with F8 collect in their own category – always within reach.
RANDOM PICK
Cannot decide? Three random games at once – and no repeats until every game has had its turn.
SEARCH
Jump straight to a game with / or F2 – matching anywhere in the name, not just the start.

VIEWS & COVER ART

THREE VIEWS – KEY F10
List, grid, gallery – separately for the game list and for the main page. F10 cycles through them, the menu remembers your default. On HDMI the grid shows 21 games at once.
GAME DESCRIPTIONS
In gallery view a description of the game sits next to the cover. It comes from the artwork database on the card – nothing to set up.
GETTING COVER ART
System → Maintenance → “Download box art” fetches it on the MiSTer itself. If you use Update All, it is often already under /media/fat/docs – the frontend reads that by itself. Not required, just convenient.
SINGLE-GAME FOLDERS
On PSX, Mega CD and Saturn each game usually sits in its own folder, because one .cue brings several .bin files with it. Those folders are dissolved – the game appears directly in the list, with cover art, grid and gallery. Multi-disc games stay folders. Can be turned off under System → Options.
PREPARING THUMBNAILS
Cover art sits on the card at full size and has to be scaled down the first time you look at it – a short wait per game. This does it in advance, once, and stores the result on the card. After that every cover is there instantly, even after a reboot. You can stop and continue later at any time.
